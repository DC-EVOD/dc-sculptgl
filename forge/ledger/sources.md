SOURCES
=======
Eleven sources. Every title and channel below was confirmed by a live oEmbed
lookup, not recalled. Character counts are `wc -c` of the files in raw/.

ZBRUSH TRACK
  163,298  zbrush-trigon-armor-OiCIxKYCRdY.txt
           "How To Create Armor in ZBRUSH - Tutorial" — TriGon
           Full chest-plate build: mask/extract, Dynamesh design exploration,
           ZRemesher-by-polygroup cleanup, surface noise passes, edge damage,
           buckles + straps modelled in Maya, stitches via curve IMM.
           The backbone of this track.

   22,864  zbrush-hristianshyne-fantasy-HfRIptAvnQ4.txt
           "HOW TO CREATE FANTASY ARMOR IN ZBRUSH" — Hristian Shyne
           Metal plate storytelling: flatten/slash/clay damage, layers +
           morph target, IMM edge trim off creased edges, chisel ornaments,
           and explicit bake-forward thinking (polygroup colour IDs).

    7,660  zbrush-gatz3d-armor-l6HokJryMkk.txt
           "Ultimate Armor Guide Part 1" — Gatz 3D
           The five-stage spine for a whole harness of ~50 pieces:
           block out, 3D sketch, split, thicken, refine.

    1,527  zbrush-royalskies-ornament-MuM75TlSBr0.txt
           "Design ORNAMENTAL Armor (In 2 Minutes!!)" — Royal Skies
           Frame Mesh by Border + curve edge brush; two-brush-type ornament
           method. Short, and one concrete free fallback (Curve Tubes).

BLENDER TRACK
  147,784  blender-abeleal-armor-dOqWAH48PoI.txt
           "Blender 5.0! Modelling and Sculpting Armor" — Abe Leal 3D
           Livestream. Heavy chat; the craft is interleaved. Museum reference,
           build-it-as-it-was-built, panel seams, thickness law, multires vs
           remesh, the centre-line asymmetry rule, Blender 5 render notes.

   44,664  blender-ironbark-worlds-yXc-Qcv6PHY.txt
           "How to Build Detailed Game Worlds in Blender" — Ironbark Games
           Modular stylised kit: radial duplication, Spin tool with exact
           values, Wall Factory's full parameter set, fractal subdivide,
           vertex-bevel chipping, ANT Landscape cliffs.

   35,843  blender-baril3d-armor-JPRuT4X_LAk.txt
           "How I make armor in Blender" — Baril3D
           The shrinkwrap panel method on greaves and a cuirass. The single
           most reusable Blender technique in this whole ledger.

   31,895  blender-baril3d-helmet-Dhl1VjIPYfw.txt
           "Model any helmet in Blender" — Baril3D
           Same method on a spangenhelm: quad-sphere base, band separation,
           vertex-group offsets, LoopTools rivet holes, curve chainmail.

           NOT captured.

COVERAGE

  first hit an extraction cap mid-word (Abe Leal at 100,000; TriGon at
  150,000); both were refetched complete and the overlap verified by matching
  the join. Nothing here is written from a partial read.
