so you have no idea how to start adding beautiful ornament designs to armor not a problem the easiest way is if you have a custom ornament brush set and you're gonna want two different types of brushes edge brushes and surface brushes i recommend you base your brush shape off of a pentagon if you don't know how to make your own custom brush you can watch my tutorial on it here or if you just want to be lazy you can grab all 56 of the ones i have personally designed for seven bucks on my art station now the first thing i would like to do is go to stroke curve functions and frame mesh by border and pick whatever curve edge brush you like and left click to create the borders if you do not have an edge brush then there is a free alternative that you can find in the defaults called curved tubes it's a little round but definitely better than nothing once you have an outline if there are any ornamental shapes that you have in your library that you would like to plaster on pick those brushes and under brush modifiers kick up the projection strength and place it where you want finally for any smaller details grab your custom design surface brush and just drag curves wherever you want do this for about two minutes and you're done it's super easy and again if you just want to use mine they're in the description hope that helps and as always hope you have a fantastic day and i'll see you around
