# PART III-B — THE DJA'INN (the full corpus)
*Forms: **Dja'Inn** (current typed) · Djinn (in the user's own old art) · Ja'in de jin · jin · jayin · Jahin · Jain · juhin · Djā'inn ↔ Dja'Ānn*

- **THE TWO-PLANE EXCLUSION LAW:** Demons cannot exist on the elemental plane. Elementals cannot exist
  in the demonic realm. *"It is not the law of nature... Every time, ends in disaster. Something
  forsaken."*

  *"like the Oni demon"*: together they would traverse both planes, *"stealing treasure, magic, power
  from both."* Elementals invoked in the telling: *"once upon an elemental. Fire elemental. Storm
  elemental. **Ancient zero**."*

  **magi planned to turn the demon into a PIECE OF ARMOUR he would wear** — to become an **Elemental
  Daemon Lord** commanding his own army of elementals. *"Both of them would just... deceive the other.
  The plan failed."*

  used caused the entities to fuse and also **halve** and also... well, they became **trapped**."*

  daemon, angels, [or] other psycho emotional entities... nor the primal, but **somewhere between**."*
  **Always bound to a physical construct AND the psycho-emotional plane that construct holds.**
  Founding vessel: **an urn.**

  to come to ruins, promising treasure"* — and on arrival *"the sand or the urn or the walls themselves
  have an elemental spirit that exists to eat your flesh. Eat your dreams. Eat whatever it can as it
  feels like that is the only way it can consume that which it misses to be free of its prison."*
- **"However, this will never free a Dja'Inn."** Devouring is **futile.**
- **THE LIBERATION CLAUSE:** *"The Dja'Inn can only be freed by that which puts them there"* — the
  **psycho-emotional energy of a mage + elemental energy.** **They must be alchemized — transmuted
  into something else — by a mage.**

  screaming for air"* — reaching oxygen inside matter *"to free it and consume it."* **AIR = FREEDOM.**
  The Dja'Inn is **fire cut off from air**, freeing-by-consuming *"because that is what fire IS."*
  Author's note: *"I didn't mean to do this when I made it a fire elemental humanoid... god that was an
  accident but mannnn that reads HARD."* And: **"I need that to be a massive part of it."**

  the consuming, that stuck in that devourer loop."** Water/earth/air manifest as **ash, dust,
  vapour.** **Air Dja'Inn are the rarest — "like plasma Dja'Inn"** — existing only where
  *"piezoelectric stones or some really electrified or zero point energy causing area has a constant
  field that binds them."* Manifestation: **"static / hissing dust of arcs."**
- **THEY TEACH NIGREDO** — *"Which means they teach negredo."* (negredo/nigredo, both kept.) The
  blackening: consumption, calcination, reduction to prima materia.
- **THE THREE USE-MODES of a bound Dja'Inn:** (1) **devourer-in-a-vessel / trap-haunt**;
  (2) **reagent** — *"elemental but psycho-emotionally charged reagents"*, spent in workings (the dark
  inversion of the liberation clause); (3) **ENGINE** — the **lantern-engines**: the **Maegi** who turn

  energy or elemental charge and force.*
