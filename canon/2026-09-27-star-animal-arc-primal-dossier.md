## CANON LOCKS (the dossier's word: "current locked direction unless later revised")
- **Star Animal is an Arc Primal expression** — not a monster form, rage mode, or external spirit animal.
- **The Arc Primals are songlines with bodies:** each is **a bestial entity, an ancestral line, AND a
  conscious dream-path** — a triune structure.
- Drohj **does not inherit a totem.** He enters the Dreaming **unplaced**, unable to name sacred site,
  ancestor or songline the way the clans expect.
- **The bestials recognise him before the humanoids understand him.** They don't embrace him, but they
  know he is animal *"by the oldest law of life, instinct, pressure, breath, and survival."*
- **His songline does not fail. It EXISTS — and goes into a "nowhere" that cannot be read by normal
  ancestral law.** *"His songline goes nowhere, but the line exists. That is what frightens them."*
- He is **heard as primordial**, because he is **unconsciously singing through their lines in his own
  thunder-voice.**
- **The Star Animal later exists as an ACTUAL CREATURE waiting for him** — not only metaphor.
- **Shoulder pylons are sound/void organs.** They **whoop, whistle, drone, and whale-call into void
  holes, cavitation scars, wormhole points, and songline ends.**

## THE ORDEAL — sequence, as recorded
The **clans are in an end-days state of fear.** They **dream of ancient clans making a sacrifice**, sense
**a star-animal is involved**, and decide any entity carrying such a dream must be dealt with — *"even if
this one is only a child or teenager."*
1. **They drug him, bind him, and throw him into a hole / crystal fall / gate / primal threshold — without
   guidance.**
2. He **should die** — from the fall, the creatures below, the drugging, or Dreaming exposure. **He does not.**
3. He enters the **Arc Primal Dreaming alone and unguided.**
4. He is **hunted, endured, witnessed, and tested** by ancient animal-songlines.
5. He **crosses primal plates / elemental thresholds in ways that should not be possible.**
6. **The Arc Primals become CONFUSED** — he has been singing through their lines without knowing it.
7. He realises **the Dreaming is thunder-song, and the same thunder lives in him.**
8. **They do not assign him a totem. They recognise him as a primordial songline-being.**
9. **They call him Star Animal** — *"because he is undeniably animal, but the star behind the animal
   cannot be placed."*

**Why they are confused — the dossier's table:** he survives **shark-pressure** (his song answers deep
predation, sensing, water-dark), **serpent-coil** (return, curve, renewal, hidden passage), **whale-record**
(distance, mourning, memory, ancient oceanic voice); he **crosses elemental thresholds** not as a trained
shaman dialling in but by **following purpose**; **his markings are ANATOMY, not tattooed language added
after birth.**
> *"They do not simply think, 'This humanoid has no totem.' They begin thinking: 'Why can this unclaimed
> thing sing through all of us?'"*

## THE DREAMING IS SOUND
*"Not pretty music, but **cacophonic harmony**."* Everything is voice: **thunder and whale-call ·
bone-rattle and claw-scrape · reptile hiss and insect clicking · blood-pulse and heartbeat · rain,
tectonic groan, pressure-hum · hunger, memory, sorrow, purpose and instinct made audible.**
- **Drohj understands because it resembles what he hears inside himself.** *"He has been hearing thunder
  inside himself the whole time, but did not know it counted as voice."*
- **Possible canon line:** *"It is all thunder. It is all song. Even the teeth. Even the dark. Even the
  things hunting me. It is all voice."*

## THE STAR ANIMAL — creature design
**A bear-sized-or-larger amphibious shark-lizard-dinosaur**, less bulky than a bear, whale/orca ancestor
feeling, dark chitin armour. Visual canon: **blueish-cyan very dark skin · black chitin armour plates ·
natural glowing runes EMBEDDED in armour/anatomy, not painted on · NO VISIBLE EYES · subtle mouth, not a
normal fish mouth · catfish sensory whiskers/barbels · head plate tapering backward over the neck ·
bulkier forelimbs capable of bracing and possibly rising onto hind legs · shoulder protrusions/pylons
like Drohj's but as ANIMAL ANATOMY · mammal/reptile/fish/isopod ambiguity · ancient shark / orca ancestor
/ land-shark lizard thing — not just a fish.** 35.246° isometric for sheets.

## PYLONS — the function, stated plainly
**"They are proto-pylon wing organs and sound/void organs. They sing into holes behind the world."**
Functions: whoop/whistle/drone/whale-call · **sound void cavities, wormhole points, forever-holes** ·
**detect dead songline ends and places where somewhere behaves like nowhere** · read gates and Dreaming
thresholds · resonate with Drohj's fallout and Wraith-glow · act as **wing-limbs, pressure horns, void
flutes, radiation antlers, and cavitation sensors.**
> *"The Star Animal did not sing to the sea. It sang to the holes behind the world. Its shoulder pylons
> whooped and whistled like ancient whales, not through water, but through void-pressure, sounding the
> forever-holes where songlines vanished."*

## ODE TO EXTINCTION — the integration
**The Ode is NOT new here.** *"Drohj's Ode to Extinction is his original antiphonic song: the resonance of
an extinct reality encoded into living incarnation. In the Arc Primal Dreaming, this ode is heard not as
abstract cosmic collapse, but as a bestial songline... His Star Animal form is therefore not separate from
the Ode to Extinction; it is the Ode becoming animal, pylon, whale-call, instinct, and primordial witness."*
> *"The Arc Primals did not give Drohj the Ode to Extinction. **They were the first living things on Vaehr
> old enough to hear it as a song.**"*

## PURPOSE-WALKING — and an explicit anti-fate ruling
Elemental thresholds normally require proper lineage or route. **Drohj crosses lines and plates in strange
ways — "his nature follows purpose through gates."**
- **IMPORTANT DISTINCTION, stated: "Drohj's motion through gates is NOT predestination."** In the broader
  descendant reincarnation structure, **earlier lives set triggers, echoes, and timed awakenings — they
  prepared opportunities and resonant conditions, NOT a fixed script.** Purpose-walking is **instinctive
  alignment with prepared echo-lines**, not fate.

## THE PREPARED-ECHO STRUCTURE (major, and new to the archive)
> *"**ALL DESCENDANTS WORKED TOGETHER IN PAST LIFETIMES** to create triggers, echoes, temple structures,
> hidden paths, and timed awakenings **to help them survive amnesia and awaken together in the final
> incarnation.**"*
- A **sacred site may be something his PAST INCARNATION hid or prepared in the Dreaming** so a later
  incarnation would naturally encounter it.
- **The descendants have internal spirit places and a shared hidden place on an overtone of the physical
  plane crossing into astral/Dreaming layers — hidden on a specific songline, functioning as a sacred
  site.** (The **dojo/temple** context.)
- **In an ancient incarnation, Drohj literally walked the earth / Dreaming AS an Arc Primal.**

## THE CLANS — wrong without being villains
*"They are operating from **ancestral law, fear, end-days pressure, and incomplete dreams.** Their mistake
is interpreting 'sacrifice of the star animal' as **removal**, when the deeper truth may be **witnessing,
transformation, returned echo, or a prepared awakening.**"* A good shaman **would know better than to
treat void-read darkness as evil** — *"but in an end-days climate, fear can win."* They **may not
understand the difference between primordial and bad darkness.**
