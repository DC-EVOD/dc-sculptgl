## 6. THE ARCHIVIST'S READING
*Curatorial prose, clearly marked. A lamp over the strata, not a hand on the scale.*

The strongest reading of this ruling is that it makes Oro'Cy **the counter-proof of the consumer law
rather than an exception to it** — and that the thing which distinguishes him is **breath.**

The reason is that the established doctrine gives fire two properties, not one. Fire consumes, *and*
fire is *"always screaming for air"* — and **air = freedom.** The Dja'Inn is terrible because it has the
first without the second: sealed in matter, no air, so the only route to freedom is devouring. That is
what makes it *"devour itself to an end."*

Oro'Cy has the air. The passage is almost entirely a breathing practice — breath into the Tail chakra,
breath through the nose that triggers no olfactory, breath *"coded with prayer"*, breath steadied in
mantra, a *"half breath"* as the unit that contains a Toroid's eternity. He is a fire that **breathes on
purpose**, and so he is a fire that does not have to consume to be free. Hence *"slow yet unbridled"* —
unbridled is not restraint, it is the absence of the cage. He burns without an end to devour himself to.

And the line that was already the most quotable in the passage now reads as elemental physics rather
than character colour: **"passion was his gateway to igniting purpose."** Under the consumer law, fire
reaching air *frees and consumes*. Oro'Cy reaching air **ignites purpose instead.** Same element, same
hunger for air, opposite outcome — the difference being that his breath is *prayer*, aimed outward into
the cavitations, at souls he cannot save. A fire whose route to freedom is intake rather than devouring.

That is also why the failure in his story does not read as defeat. A consuming fire that fails has
nothing left; it has spent itself. A breathing fire that fails is still burning, *"still sensitive"*, and
can stay — which is exactly what he does, waiting for the fallout to subside so that anything at all can
incarnate again.

If that reading is right, the pairing to keep is **Dja'Inn : Oro'Cy :: fire bound : fire breathing** —
and the Dark-Light Templar who hands him a third heart *outside his own chest* is doing the same thing
by other means: giving a starved thing an outside to reach.
