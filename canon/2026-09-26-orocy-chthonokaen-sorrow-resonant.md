## 6. THE ARCHIVIST'S READING
*Clearly marked as curatorial prose. A lamp held over the strata, not a hand on the scale.*

The strongest reading of this passage is that it is **the origin record of the CALL**, and that its
real subject is not Oro'Cy's failure but **what an instrument built for prevention becomes once
prevention is already lost.**

The reason is structural, not tonal. Oro'Cy grows an organ — deliberately, patiently, trained *like a
plant* — to be "another edge on the sword of tempered instincts that would prevent this age of
extinction." The extinction happens anyway. The organ does not stop working; it **inverts**. Sheathed
but still sensitive, it now transmits exactly the catastrophe it was grown to avert, in full fidelity,
through his whole nervous system. That is the passage's engine, and it rhymes hard with the archive's
own thesis that descent is sacrament rather than transgression: the failed guardian is not discarded,
he is **converted into the instrument that can register what happened** — the only witness, staying on
purpose for a dawn he may not see.

Drohj's answer is the counterweight, and it is why this belongs next to the Zeal material rather than
merely beside it. He does not console and does not repair. He **re-counts** — *"You have more than two
hearts"*, *"more than one voice"* — and locates the third heart **outside Oro'Cy's own chest, in his
own**, with orbital ordnance behind stars that follow his gravity wherever he is falling. The remedy for
a guardian who failed alone is not more capacity; it is a heart he cannot hold himself, that answers
when called. And what it yields is named in the text: not rescue, not vengeance — **"calm defiance,"**
an already-established Zeal-state, here given its birth scene at death's precipice.

The convergence that most deserves David's eye is the **Omen → Primeval Fear → Cavitation** sequence in
the mirage. Those three are already canon as *pre-utterance states*, and they arrive here in order, in
the moment before Drohj's chant. If that was deliberate, this passage is a working demonstration of the
pre-utterance grammar. If it was not, it is **convergent evidence that the grammar is load-bearing** —
that it surfaces unbidden when the author writes at depth. Either way it is the strongest single thing
in the file, and it is his to rule on.
