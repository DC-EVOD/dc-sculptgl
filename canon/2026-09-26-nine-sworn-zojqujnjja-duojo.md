## 8. THE ARCHIVIST'S READING
*Curatorial prose, clearly marked. A lamp over the strata, not a hand on the scale.*

The strongest reading of this ruling is that it makes **the Nine-Descendant synergy structure into a
family, and Drohj's loneliness into the shape of the story.**

The reason is what the archive already says the synergy *does.* Each Descendant interaction
simultaneously reveals a facet of Drohj, awakens that Descendant's mastery, and seeds an archetype
into Vaehr's consciousness field. Read against nine *brothers*, that is not a quest structure — it is
**a man recovering his family one at a time, and getting a piece of himself back with each one.** The
mastery he awakens in them is the facet he recovers in himself. Nine brothers, nine facets, one
scattered being.

And "not real brothers, just those of the Du'Ojo" is the load-bearing clause. It means the kinship is
**made, not inherited** — which is precisely the half-name mechanic applied to family: *earned
meaning, differentiated union*, set against the dark pyramid's assigned meaning. Blood would be
assigned. The Du'Ojo is earned. That is why the archive's own doctrine keeps insisting union is
*differentiated* rather than homogenous — nine who are brothers without being the same.

If the mask reading also holds — the same name carrying *the art of masks* — then the nine are a set
of faces, and Drohj, the hooded one whose face is a void, is the brother whose mask is absence. Which
would make Oro'Cy, who has **no eyes at all** and wears a **Red-Cloth mask** he pulls down to breathe,
a very particular kind of candidate.

That last step is mine, not yours, and it is flagged as such.
